Academic Framework

Mount Litera IntraMUN is designed as a disciplined academic forum where students engage with international affairs, national policy questions, lawmaking, negotiation, and written documentation.

Purpose: The conference develops research ability, formal speech, collaboration, procedural discipline, and critical thinking. Delegates are expected to maintain decorum, substantiate claims, respect committee rules, and participate constructively throughout the simulation.

Conference Flow

The two-day format moves delegates from opening positions to negotiated outcomes, crisis responses, committee documents, and final voting.

Day 1: Positioning and Development

  • Opening ceremony, committee briefing, and roll call.
  • General Speakers List and opening policy statements.
  • Moderated caucuses to examine focused sub-issues.
  • Unmoderated caucuses for bloc formation and document planning.
  • Committee-specific crisis updates, questions, or procedural interventions.

Day 2: Drafting and Voting

  • Review of previous committee decisions and updated developments.
  • Drafting, merging, and refinement of committee documents.
  • Formal amendment process and substantive debate.
  • Roll-call voting or parliamentary voting as applicable.
  • Closing ceremony, feedback, and awards.

Continuous Crisis Committee

CCC

A committee built for strategic decision-making under pressure.

Agenda

The Commission Under Siege: Evading the FBI, Securing Syndicate Rackets, and Internal Underworld Turf Wars.

Context

Set in 1931, this committee examines a fictionalized historical crisis around organized criminal networks, law enforcement pressure, resource control, and internal distrust. Delegates must balance public negotiation with private strategy while responding to rapidly changing developments from the Executive Board.

Procedure

  • General Speakers List: Delegates use formal speeches to state strategy, propose alliances, and influence the direction of the room.
  • Crisis Chits: Delegates may submit private actions to the crisis staff. Responses determine whether actions succeed, fail, or trigger further developments.
  • Public Directives: Delegates draft concise action documents to respond to urgent committee updates.

United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime

UNODC

A policy committee focused on international cooperation and enforceable frameworks.

Agenda

Dismantling Dark Web Narcotic Supply Chains and Neutralizing Decentralized Crypto-Laundering Networks.

Context

Transnational criminal networks increasingly use encrypted marketplaces and decentralized digital assets to evade traditional law enforcement. The committee must address jurisdiction, evidence sharing, financial tracing, server takedowns, and safeguards for lawful digital activity.

Procedure

  • General Speakers List: Delegates present national positions on cybercrime, narcotics enforcement, data sharing, and financial regulation.
  • Committee Chits: Delegates coordinate with allies, request information, and organize drafting blocs.
  • Draft Resolutions: Delegates prepare, debate, amend, and vote on international policy recommendations.

Parliament of India

Lok Sabha

A parliamentary simulation based on legislative debate and ministerial accountability.

Agenda

Discussion on the Management of National Competitive Examinations with Special Emphasis on Urban Infrastructure Support, Public Mobilization Disruptions, and Ensuring Student Access in State Capitals.

Context

The committee considers how public mobilizations, administrative planning, traffic control, and examination schedules interact with student access to national competitive examinations. Members must examine accountability across state authorities, municipal bodies, and central testing agencies.

Procedure

  • Leaders' Addresses: Government and Opposition members present their formal positions and frame the debate.
  • Question Hour and Zero Hour: Members raise urgent issues, demand accountability, and respond to direct questions.
  • Parliamentary Chits: Members coordinate strategy, raise procedural concerns, or request Speaker intervention.
  • Bill Procedure: The House debates amendments and votes on the Competitive Examinations Public Transit and Safeguards Bill.

United Nations Cyber Security

UNCBS

A committee on digital sovereignty, infrastructure protection, and cyber conflict norms.

Agenda

Securing Global Critical Infrastructure Against State-Sponsored Ransomware and Establishing Rules of Engagement for Sovereign Cyber Warfare.

Context

Cyberattacks against hospitals, transport systems, power grids, and water networks can disrupt public life without crossing a physical border. Delegates must define thresholds, attribution standards, proportional response, international cooperation, and protections for civilian infrastructure.

Procedure

  • Threat Assessment: Delegates present national cyber readiness, recent threats, and policy priorities.
  • System Chits: Delegates request technical updates from the Executive Board about simulated network conditions.
  • Joint Cyber Directives: Delegates coordinate urgent responses to simulated attacks or infrastructure breaches.
  • Cyber-Accord Treaty: Delegates draft a final international framework on cyber conduct and response.
